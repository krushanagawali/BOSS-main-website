Built on a Zero-Knowledge Architecture

At Bharat Online Safety Services, we believe your personal documents are yours alone. Our core infrastructure is engineered around a strict zero-database philosophy.

Unlike traditional cloud printing services that store your files indefinitely, our routing system holds your data only as encrypted packets in transit. We cannot read, store, or sell your documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents saved on your servers?

No. Our zero-database architecture means your files are held in temporary memory just long enough to reach the printer, after which they are permanently destroyed.

How long is my 6-digit code valid?

Codes are valid for a strictly limited active session to prevent unauthorized fetching.

Can shopkeepers reprint my documents later?

No. Once the document is fetched and sent to the printer queue, the encrypted payload is wiped from the shopkeeper terminal.
